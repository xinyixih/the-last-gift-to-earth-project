Welcome

This toolkit helps any organization host a 45-minute session on green burial and end-of-life choices for young adults and seniors. You don't need to be an expert on death care.

At a glance

  • Session length: about 45 minutes
  • Group size: 8–30 people
  • Cost: free, plus optional materials for the diorama

How to get started

  1. Read the Facilitator Guide (folder 01).
  2. Review the slides and choose an activity.
  3. Print the handouts and set up the surveys.
  4. Run your session, and send us your feedback!
